# cookidea-github-source
CookIdea

CookIdea is an AI-powered brainstorming workspace that transforms idea generation into a creative cooking experience.

Instead of chatting with AI through prompts, users “cook” ideas by dragging ingredients—such as skills, goals, target users, pain points, resources, constraints, and scenarios—into an interactive Idea Pot. Acting like a creative chef, the AI analyzes the relationships between these ingredients, automatically recommends complementary “seasonings,” explores multiple combinations, and generates actionable product ideas, startup directions, research topics, and execution plans.

CookIdea is built around a simple philosophy:

Great ideas are not generated—they are cooked.

By turning abstract thinking into an intuitive visual workflow, CookIdea helps entrepreneurs, students, researchers, designers, and creators organize fragmented thoughts into structured, practical solutions. Rather than functioning as a traditional chatbot, it introduces a new interaction paradigm where humans and AI co-create through a playful yet productive creative process.

✨ Core Features

🥘 Drag-and-drop ingredient-based brainstorming
🧠 AI-powered knowledge graph and idea synthesis
🔥 Creativity levels with different “cooking heat” modes
🧂 Intelligent recommendation of complementary ingredients
📖 Recipe-style outputs with MVP roadmaps and business suggestions
🧊 Personal “Fridge” for reusable skills, resources, and goals
🎮 Immersive 3D cooking workspace powered by modern web technologies

CookIdea aims to make innovation feel as natural and enjoyable as cooking—helping anyone transform scattered inspiration into ideas that can actually be built.

CookIdea

CookIdea 是一款以“创意烹饪”为核心隐喻的 AI Brainstorm 工作空间。

它将创意构思过程转化为一次充满乐趣的“做菜”体验。

用户不再通过输入 Prompt 与 AI 对话，而是将自己的技能、目标、目标用户、市场痛点、已有资源、限制条件以及应用场景等抽象信息，以“食材”的形式拖拽进一口互动式的 Idea Pot（创意锅）。AI 则像一位创意主厨，分析不同食材之间的关联关系，自动补充合适的“调料”，探索多种组合方式，最终生成可执行的产品创意、创业方向、科研选题以及落地方案。

CookIdea 建立在一个简单却直观的理念之上：

伟大的创意不是凭空生成的，而是一步步“烹饪”出来的。

通过将抽象的思考过程可视化、游戏化，CookIdea 帮助创业者、学生、研究人员、设计师以及内容创作者，把零散的灵感组织成系统化、可执行的解决方案。它并不是传统意义上的聊天机器人，而是一种全新的创意交互范式——让人与 AI 像共同下厨一样，共同完成创意构思。

✨ 核心特性

🥘 基于拖拽“食材”的可视化 Brainstorm
🧠 基于知识图谱的 AI 创意推理与组合
🔥 不同“火候”模式带来的多层次创造力探索
🧂 智能推荐辅助食材，自动补全创意链路
📖 以“菜谱”形式输出产品方案、MVP 路线和商业建议
🧊 个人「冰箱」系统，用于长期保存技能、资源和目标
🎮 基于现代 Web 技术打造的沉浸式 3D 创意工作空间

CookIdea 希望让创新像做饭一样自然、有趣，让每个人都能把零散的灵感一步步烹饪成真正可以落地的创意。
